name: shared-layer
description: The team's shared knowledge base (shared-layer MCP server). Use to look up or save knowledge about clients, projects, meetings, decisions, processes or people.
---

The `shared-layer` MCP server holds the team's shared knowledge base: what the team knows and decides about clients, projects, people, meetings, decisions and processes. Agents collect knowledge into it, store it as markdown concepts in the right area and folder, keep it up to date, and read it back with citations. It is split into areas (team areas, and a personal area per person), each organized by the rules in its folders' `index.md`.

Before any other `shared-layer` tool, call its `begin` tool once per session and follow what it returns: how the knowledge base is organized, how to read, write and maintain knowledge, and the root index with the rules and the areas you can access.
