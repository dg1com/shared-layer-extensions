## Shared knowledge base

The `shared-layer` MCP server is the team's shared knowledge base, also called the shared layer, the KB, team memory, the wiki or the knowledge database. Use it to recall what the team knows or has decided about clients, projects, people, meetings, decisions and processes: check it before answering such questions from memory or the web. Use it to save such knowledge when asked to remember, store or note something down for the team or for later. Notes and conventions for one repository belong in that repository's own files instead.

Before any other of its tools, call its `begin` tool once per session and follow what it returns: how the knowledge base is organized and used, and the root index with its rules and the areas you can access.

It also reaches other services, such as Linear, Notion or Google, through connections people connected in the Shared Layer app and shared with you. `begin` lists your connections by handle, not their tools: find a tool with `connection_search` (say what you want to do), then run it with `connection_run` and the handle. Write only what the person asked for, and say which connection you used. When a tool is refused, tell the person what the error says.
